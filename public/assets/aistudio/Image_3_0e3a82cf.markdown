## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an elite, credentialed visual identity tailored for executive education partnerships between premier management institutes and global professional bodies. It speaks to mid-to-senior HR leaders and enterprise decision-makers seeking career acceleration through rigorous, accredited post-graduate certifications.

### Aesthetic Philosophy
The visual direction fuses academic gravitas with contemporary executive software precision:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, rigorous, prestigious, yet agile and forward-looking.
- **Design Style:** Modern Corporate meets Precision Editorial. It relies on structured micro-rhythms, sharp typography, architectural alignments, and restrained tonal contrast rather than playful decoration.
- **Trust Architecture:** Unapologetic emphasis on institutional legitimacy. Partner lockups, accreditation ribbons, ranking badges, and faculty endorsements function as core visual anchors rather than peripheral footnotes.
- **Focal Tension:** The deliberate collision of deep, institutional teals against high-visibility conversion accents (lime green) ensures high-impact application conversions without diluting Tier-1 prestige.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional pedigree with conversion mechanics:

### Core Roles
- **Primary Institutional Teal (`#005A5B`):** The foundational brand anchor used for primary brand touchpoints, dominant headers, focused states, and structural navigation.
- **Dark Forest Teal (`#003B3C`):** High-density contrast background for executive hero sections, premium credential cards, and anchor headers. Conveys heritage and academic rigor.
- **Accent Conversion Lime (`#84CC16`):** Precision performance accent reserved strictly for high-conversion CTAs, active status chips, key timeline anchors, and application deadlines. Paired with `#003B3C` text for AA/AAA compliance.
- **Slate Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Low-strain, cool-tinted neutral surface that eliminates pure-white glare and provides subtle contrast against white card surfaces.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity elevated surface for curriculum modules, faculty bios, fee schedules, and interactive lead capture forms.

### Secondary Tonal Tokens
- **Border Subtle:** `#E2E8F0` for clean card boundaries.
- **Border Institutional:** `#005A5B` at 15% opacity (`rgba(0, 90, 91, 0.15)`) for academic credential containers.
- **Text Main:** `#0F172A` (Slate 900) for highest readability body text.
- **Text Muted:** `#475569` (Slate 600) for supporting copy, cohort schedules, and metadata.

## Typography

Typography is systematic, modern, and calibrated for dense academic documentation alongside conversion-driven executive landing surfaces.

### Rationale & Rules
- **Inter Everywhere:** The design system deploys `Inter` across display, body, and micro-labels to maintain structural clarity and high legibility across digital viewports.
- **Negative Letter Spacing on Scale:** Headings utilize tight, negative tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.01em`) to create dense, impactful, newspaper-grade executive titles.
- **Case Differentiation:** Institutional badges, cohort labels, and accreditation metadata use uppercase styling coupled with `label-sm` or `label-md` and expanded letter tracking (`+0.05em`).
- **Tabular Figures:** Numbers in program fees, cohort dates, batch size metrics, and curriculum module durations must render using tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for precise visual alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored to a structured 12-column grid designed for executive consumption with compact vertical density.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, max-width `1280px`, centered. Section margins at `3rem` (`margin`), column gutters at `1.5rem` (`gutter`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margins (`margin-mobile`) and `1rem` gutters (`gutter-mobile`).

### Vertical Rhythm
- Spacing is restrained and intentional. Content-dense academic syllabi and executive summaries avoid gratuitous padding to minimize executive scroll fatigue.
- Related elements (e.g., trust badges and partner logos) use tight clustering (`space-sm` to `space-md`), while programmatic blocks separate cleanly using `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through tonal layering and low-contrast perimeter definition rather than exaggerated drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Base canvas background (`#F8FAFC`). No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card Default):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a micro-stroke (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient, low-opacity shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Executive Container / Interactive Hover):** Elevated cards on hover and sticky application bars: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(0, 59, 60, 0.07), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(0, 59, 60, 0.04)`. Note the subtle tinting using the Dark Forest Teal tone.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Modals):** Brochure download modals, eligibility calculators: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(0, 30, 31, 0.18), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(0, 30, 31, 0.10)`.

### Dark Contrast Cards
For dark containers utilizing `#003B3C`, depth is conveyed strictly via interior border definition: an inner stroke of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` without exterior drop shadow.

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, soft corner radius (Level 1) that communicates institutional stability and executive precision.

### Application Rules
- **Base Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Tabs):** `0.25rem` (4px). Clean, crisp corners that feel structured rather than playful.
- **Cards, Curriculum Accordions, Feature Containers:** `0.5rem` (8px). Provides enough softness for contemporary polish while avoiding consumer-grade pill shapes.
- **Accreditation Marks & Cohort Pills:** Small badge containers strictly use `0.25rem` (4px) with crisp 1px borders, preserving the feel of formal certificates and official stamps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Conversion):** Background `#84CC16`, text `#003B3C`, weight 600, height 48px, horizontal padding `1.5rem`, border-radius 4px. On hover: background `#70B614`. On focus: 2px ring in `#005A5B` with 2px offset.
- **Institutional Primary:** Background `#005A5B`, text `#FFFFFF`, weight 600. On hover: background `#003B3C`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `1px solid #005A5B`, text `#005A5B`. On dark containers: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Chips & Trust Badges
- **Accreditation Pill:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, typography `label-sm`, uppercase. Includes grayscale-to-brand logo lockup for AACSB and EQUIS.
- **Batch Status Badge (e.g., "Batch 07 — Enrolling Now"):** Background `rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.15)`, text `#003B3C`, border `1px solid #84CC16`, border-radius 4px, padding `0.25rem 0.5rem`.

### Form Fields (Lead Capture & Inquiry)
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, height 44px, radius 4px, font `body-md`. Focus state: border `#005A5B`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 90, 91, 0.12)`. Labels use `label-md` in `#334155`.

### Cards & Module Expanders
- **Curriculum Card:** Pure white surface, 1px slate border (`#E2E8F0`), padding `1.5rem`. Left-edge 3px indicator strip in `#005A5B` for active module.
- **Executive Contrast Card:** Background `#003B3C`, interior padding `2rem`, text `#F8FAFC`, featuring program fee breakdown, scholarship tiers, and direct admissions contact points.

### Institutional Partner Lockup
- Horizontal dual-anchor layout pairing IIM Shillong's heraldic mark with the SHRM India credential. Separated by a neutral hairline divider (`1px solid #CBD5E1`) with fixed height 32px, ensuring balanced optical weight.
